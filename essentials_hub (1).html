Interactive Algebra

Isolating variables to identify hidden unknowns inside balanced formulas.

3x + 7 = 22
  • Step 1 (Isolate modifier): Subtract 7 from both sides → 3x = 22 - 7
  • Step 2 (Simplify expression): 3x = 15
  • Step 3 (Extract unknown x): Divide by 3 → x = 15 / 3 → x = 5
